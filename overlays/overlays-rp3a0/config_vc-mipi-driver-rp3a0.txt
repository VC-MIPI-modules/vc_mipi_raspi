###      | IMX412, IMX415, IMX462, IMX565, IMX566, IMX567, IMX568, IMX900
### ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
###  Sensor manufacturer
### -----+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### Omnivision (OV7251,OV9281)    => cam0_manu_ov
### Sony(IMX...       )           => cam0_manu_sony
